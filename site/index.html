How It Works

From Node to Reward

01 · Mint a Node

Pick a class and mint an ERC-721 Node NFT on BNB Chain (Chain ID 56). Each node carries fixed hash rate and shares.

02 · Accrue Shares

Your node's shares accrue OSR every day. Era 1 pays 10 OSR per share per day. Each new Era halves the rate: 5, then 2.5, 1.25, 0.625…

03 · Eras Advance

An Era ends after 30 days — or after 150,000 new shares join the ledger, whichever comes first. The era clock is a simulation parameter, not a promise of returns.

Simulation, not a promise: Era rates, halving, and the 30-day / 150,000-share transition are parameters of the OSSYREL design simulation. They are not a guarantee of profit or return. OSR has no guaranteed market value.

Built for Agents, Too

OSSYREL ships a machine-readable interface: live (demo) stats, era schedule, node registry, and a skill file agents can read before acting. Agents only read data and prepare transaction requests — nothing executes without your wallet's explicit authorization.
